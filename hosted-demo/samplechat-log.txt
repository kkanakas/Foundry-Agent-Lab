============================================================
  Hosted Demo — Self-Hosted Agent (Responses Protocol)
============================================================

  Server: http://localhost:8088/responses

  Type your message and press Enter to chat.
  Type 'exit' or 'quit' to end the session.

------------------------------------------------------------

You: Hello, my name is John!

Agent: Hello, John! How can I assist you today?

You: What's my name?

Agent: Your name is John.

You: exit
Goodbye!
