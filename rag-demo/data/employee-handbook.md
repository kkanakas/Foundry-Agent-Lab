# Contoso Electronics - Employee Handbook (Sample)

## Company Overview

Contoso Electronics is a global leader in consumer electronics, founded in 1985.
Headquartered in Redmond, WA, we employ over 12,000 people across 30 countries.

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
All full-time employees receive comprehensive health coverage starting on day one.
Plans include medical, dental, and vision. Dependents are covered at no extra cost
for the basic plan. Premium plans are available with a $50/month employee contribution.

### Paid Time Off
- New employees: 15 days PTO per year
- After 3 years: 20 days PTO per year
- After 7 years: 25 days PTO per year
- Holidays: 10 company-observed holidays per year
- Sick leave: Unlimited (manager discretion)

### 401(k) Retirement Plan
Contoso matches 100% of employee contributions up to 6% of salary.
Vesting schedule: immediate for employee contributions, 3-year cliff for employer match.

### Education Assistance
Up to $5,250 per year for job-related education. Covers tuition, books, and fees.
Must maintain a B average. Pre-approval required from your manager.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Core hours: 10 AM - 3 PM local time (for meetings and collaboration).
Equipment: Company provides laptop, monitor, and $500 home office stipend.

## Performance Reviews

- Annual reviews in Q4 (October-December)
- Mid-year check-ins in Q2 (April-June)
- Rating scale: 1 (Needs Improvement) to 5 (Exceptional)
- Bonus eligibility: Rating of 3 or above
- Promotion eligibility: Rating of 4 or above for two consecutive years

## IT Support

- Help desk: help@contoso.com or ext. 4357
- Hours: 24/7 for critical issues, 8 AM - 6 PM for general requests
- Password resets: Self-service at https://password.contoso.com
- New equipment requests: Submit through ServiceNow portal
- VPN: Required for remote access to internal systems

## Travel Policy

- Domestic flights: Economy class, book 14+ days in advance
- International flights: Business class for flights over 6 hours
- Hotels: Up to $200/night domestic, $300/night international
- Meals: $75/day domestic, $100/day international
- All travel requires pre-approval for trips over $1,000
